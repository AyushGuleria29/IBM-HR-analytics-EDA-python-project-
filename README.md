# IBM HR Analytics - Employee Attrition & Performance EDA

This repository contains an exploratory data analysis (EDA) project focused on understanding the factors that drive employee attrition and retention using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

## 📁 Project Structure

* `IBM_HR_Analytics_EDA_Project.ipynb`: The primary Jupyter Notebook containing data cleaning, preprocessing, visualizations, and insights.

## 🚀 Key Features & Analysis

* **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**: Inspected the dataset for missing values, handled categorical variable encoding, and dropped redundant/constant columns.
* **Univariate Analysis**: Explored individual feature distributions such as employee age, monthly income, job satisfaction, distance from home, and years at the company.
* **Bivariate Analysis**: Investigated relationships between employee attrition and key workplace factors including OverTime, Job Level, Work-Life Balance, and Total Working Years.
* **Data Visualization**: Generated insightful distribution plots, correlation heatmaps, and categorical charts using Python visualization libraries.

## 📊 Key Insights

* **OverTime Impact**: Employees working overtime showed a significantly higher attrition rate compared to those who did not.
* **Income & Job Level**: Lower monthly income and entry-level positions strongly correlated with higher employee turnover.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Dissatisfaction with work-life balance and lower job satisfaction levels were notable contributors to departures.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Pandas & NumPy** (Data manipulation and analysis)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Data visualization)
* **Jupyter Notebook** (Interactive development environment)
